Rep0rt share what would otherwise be lost

The results science throws away.

You spent months on a result that didn't confirm the hypothesis. It won't get published. It won't count. And somewhere, another researcher is about to spend their months on the exact same dead end.

Rep0rt gives those results a home: an open, free, and citable record of what didn't work, so the field stops repeating it.

3

Others build on it

Researchers find it, cite it, and skip the dead end you already mapped out.

Every result has the bones of a paper, even the ones that never get published.
